LAYER 4 · GUARDIAN VERIFICATION

What your trusted contact sees

Alerts arrive in two tiers. A soft notice says only "couldn't confirm she's okay" and carries the context that explains it. A full alert is the real thing. Either way a human decides before authorities are involved.

The honest problem with "AI detects distress"

No classifier can reliably tell an angry scream at your sibling from a terrified scream at an attacker. Audio and motion do not carry that information.

So we stopped trying to build a perfect detector, and built a system that stays safe when the detector is wrong.

Context can be wrong too — five safeguards

1 · Threshold capped at 85. Suppression raises the bar; it never closes the door.

2 · One person is not a crowd. A single suppression-eligible device counts far less than several.

3 · Suppression is opt-in per contact. Guardian-eligible ≠ suppression-eligible.

4 · Repeat-suppression override. Three suppressed events in the window discard suppression entirely — the pattern is the signal.

5 · Manual trigger bypasses everything, pocket-operable, in any mode.

Answering is not one gesture

The premise is "inability to act signals danger" — so it is wrong to define acting as one narrow tap. Unlocking your phone normally proves you are conscious, free and in control of your device. So do a spoken safe word, a shake, or a volume sequence.

Five channels make the false alarms disappear and make the real signal stronger: silence across all five is far better evidence of genuine inability than one missed tap.

The countdown also scales with context — 15 s when pre-armed and seconds matter, up to 60 s at home where they do not, and longer still while riding.

Riding versus being taken

Bad roads produce exactly the signature the detector looks for: hard motion plus loud ambient noise. But riding is sustained, rhythmic, and travelling at 30+ km/h in a consistent direction — nothing like a few seconds of chaos. So transit gets its own mode where motion stops counting and crash detection takes over.

The trap: an abduction is also a vehicle journey. So ride mode has to be earned, not granted.

The system asks not "is this a vehicle?" but "how did this journey begin?" Walking, then driving, nothing outstanding → clean → ride mode. A distress event or an unanswered check, then driving → dirty → abduction watch: threshold to the floor, guardians already alerted, and location streaming as a live trail rather than a single pin. In an abduction the most valuable output is not an alarm — it is a track police can follow.

Where the system genuinely ends

A quiet abduction — no scream, no struggle, phone kept, victim walked away under threat — leaves the detector nothing to detect. We do not claim otherwise.

What remains: the discreet manual trigger, and behavioural evidence — off her usual route, at an odd hour, or a journey whose ETA passed without arrival. Those trigger a proof-of-life check. Silence then escalates.

Likewise, if the attacker destroys the phone, no software fixes that — a continuous trail up to that moment is the honest best. That is the real argument for a companion wearable, and a roadmap item rather than a hidden gap.

Running this on stage

Microphone, motion and vibration only work on a secure origin — that means https:// or localhost. Opening this over your laptop's LAN address (http://192.168.x.x:8000) will silently block the mic and motion sensors. Do not rely on it.

Best — real phone, full sensors: go to app.netlify.com/drop, drag this single file in, and you get an HTTPS link in about thirty seconds. Open that on your phone. No account or build step needed.

Alternative: cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:8000 wraps a local server in a public HTTPS URL, so you can keep editing the file live.

Fallback if the venue Wi-Fi fails: run python3 -m http.server 8000 and open http://localhost:8000 on the laptop itself — localhost counts as secure, so the mic works. Laptops have no accelerometer, so drive motion with the inject buttons. This also projects better.

Use an Android phone if you can. iOS Safari does not support the vibration API, so the silent buzz will not fire there — the on-screen countdown still does everything else.

The Guardian tab is part of this same app — there is no backend, so two phones will not sync. Demo it by switching tabs on one device, ideally mirrored to the projector.

The ladder

Sensors: mic · accelerometer · GPS speed & geofence · BLE proximity · activity recognition
↓
Layer 1 — Context Engine. Place, time, route, opt-in nearby devices, movement state → dynamic threshold, capped at 85.
↓
Transit entry classifier. Clean (walking → driving, nothing outstanding) → ride mode: motion discounted, crash detection on. Dirty (event or unanswered check → driving) → abduction watch: threshold to floor, live location trail, guardians alerted.
↓
Layer 2 — Detection. Dual-signal fusion evaluated on the event's peak over an 800 ms window, not the rising edge. Outputs a score only.
↓
Gate. Below threshold → suppressed. Three suppressions in the window → override and open the sentinel anyway.
↓
Layer 3 — Sentinel Check. Context-scaled window. Five proof-of-life channels: tap pattern, phone unlock, safe word, shake, volume sequence. Duress pattern → fake cancel + silent escalation.
↓
Layer 4 — Guardian Verification. Soft notice ("couldn't confirm") auto-promotes to full alert if unresolved. Location, audio, classifier hint, context line, live trail. Human confirms before authorities. Recall window cancels within seconds.
↓
Manual trigger bypasses Layers 1–3 entirely, in any mode.

Real vs. mocked

Real: microphone amplitude sensing, device-motion sensing, GPS, the entire state machine — context scoring and cap, repeat-override, transit entry classification, ride mode motion discounting, abduction watch, crash path, all five proof-of-life channels, context-scaled windows, soft→full promotion, recall, journey watch, route anomaly, live trail accumulation, audio capture, and the guardian confirm/dismiss flow.

Simulated by buttons: BLE proximity, GPS speed and activity recognition (production uses Android ActivityRecognition / iOS CMMotionActivity), the audio event classifier's label, the guardian roster, and phone power-off. Timings are shortened for stage: override window 3 min (spec 20), journey ETA 20 s, soft→full promotion 20 s (spec 60). Say this to judges — it reads as engineering honesty.
